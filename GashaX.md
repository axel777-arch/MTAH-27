# GashaX — Phase 1 Technical Note

## Approach

GashaX's submission is generated by a deterministic, tracking-first Python inference pipeline in
[`starter/possession_model.py`](starter/possession_model.py). It is a rule-based temporal system,
not a trained machine-learning model. No LightGBM/XGBoost model was trained because no permitted
external labeled training dataset was supplied.

The pipeline uses:

- The tactical Automatic Tracking Data (ATD) text file for player/ball locations and speeds.
- The tactical ATD metadata XML to map tracking slots to the home and away teams.
- The tactical Smart Tagging XML for set-piece intervals only.

The video and possession ground-truth labels are not inputs to the implementation. Tracking and
tagging are automatic and noisy, so their outputs are treated as evidence rather than truth.

## Algorithm

1. Read the tactical ATD in order and verify each record has the expected contiguous 1-based frame
   ID and metadata-defined number of player slots.
2. For each frame with a detected ball, compute each team's nearest tracked-player distance in
   metres, scaling normalized x and y by the pitch dimensions separately.
3. Consider a team a possible controller only when its nearest player is within 2.0 m of the ball
   and at least 0.5 m closer than the opposing team's nearest player.
4. Require 10 qualifying observations before assigning or changing the possessor. Allow up to two
   ambiguous/missing candidate frames during a pending change. This temporal persistence is
   intended to suppress single-frame flicker and isolated touches.
5. Keep the current possessor through ball-only tracking gaps. Output `X` when no owner has been
   established or when player tracking disappears for a sustained interval.
6. Output `D` when the ball is outside the normalized pitch boundary by more than 0.005 for at
   least three consecutive frames, or is moving at most 0.2 m/s inside an automatically tagged
   set-piece interval for at least five consecutive frames. Once confirmed, label the full evidence
   run `D`. Requiring persistence avoids reacting to one-frame tracking spikes or brief slowdowns,
   but can miss short stoppages.
7. Reset possession after a detected dead-ball cue. A team must establish control again after play
   resumes.

Labels are `H` (Barcelona), `A` (Athletic Club), `D` (dead ball), and `X` (abstention). The CSV
contains one row for every tactical-video frame, from frame 0 through frame 199318.

## Data handling and compliance

The submission timeline is the tactical recording. ATD frame IDs are converted with
`video_frame = frame_id - 1`; Smart Tagging times are converted with `round(seconds * 25)`.
Missing ATD values remain missing and are not interpreted as dead ball by themselves.

The organizer's clarification prohibits checking predictions against the video and training,
fitting, calibrating, or tuning on the provided video or possession ground truth. This system uses
fixed heuristics, not fitted parameters. The public five-minute evaluator was run once as a
report-only check (spellF1 0.4588, macroF1 0.5679 on 17 possessions); the result did not guide model
selection or tuning. Match data must not be committed to the public repository, redistributed, or
uploaded to hosted AI services.

## Limitations

Nearest-player distance is only a proxy for control. ATD tracklets can fragment or switch identity;
the ball is often missing; Smart Tagging can be wrong; and the dead-ball cues do not represent every
referee stoppage. The thresholds are engineering assumptions, not learned or validated probabilities.
No accuracy or competition-score claim is made. The checks below establish synthetic behavior,
CSV format, and deterministic regeneration only.

## Reproduction

With the authorized match bundle unpacked under `data/` (or selected with `MTAH_DATA_DIR`), run:

```bash
python starter/possession_model.py --out evaluation/results/GashaX.csv
python evaluation/validate_submission.py evaluation/results/GashaX.csv
python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

The validator checks the exact submission format; it does not assess label correctness. The
generated CSV is git-ignored and should be uploaded through the competition form, not committed to
the public repository. Dependencies and implementation details are documented in
[`MODEL_PROCESS.md`](MODEL_PROCESS.md).
